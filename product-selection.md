Our team KineticKittens has chosen the degree planner, we chose this product because it is something we are all familiar with, doing our own degree planning, much of it using pen and paper. The ability to view digitally differing pathways to completing a degree depending on what has been taken, what is required, and more. That is something that most college students value, and especially those students navigating college for the first time.

Our second choice was the async standup & sprint board. Utilizing messaging tools such as Discord and Slack, we all have some experience within this area. We ultimately decided against this product due to the fact that a degree planner calls more to our current skillset.

Worries that we have as a team are personal time constraints. Each team member works differing schedules, as well as personal obligations. We also have worries of inexperience with code and our abilities to contribute effectively. With these worries in mind, we are all willing to do what we can to complete tasks together, and to help one another out. Time constraints we will work around by having flexible personal code working hours, as well as an expected response time of 5 hours. For code, we are willing to learn and adapt, as well as teach one another to help further the team as a whole.
